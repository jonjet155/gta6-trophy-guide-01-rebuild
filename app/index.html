⚠ THE ONLY MISSABLE TROPHY — DO THIS FIRST

Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at 18 spots in 10 locations, as Jason and Lucia. Lucia’s balcony meditation is only open in Chapter 1 and vanishes when the chapter ends. There is no mission replay, so if you miss it, the platinum needs a fresh playthrough.

Save discipline: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one before the final mission. Sources: GTABase; PowerPyx; outputlag.

Start here: the road to the platinum

Do the stages in order. The roadmap is built from the official trophy descriptions: chapters, missions, collectibles and counts, in the order they open.

  1. 1

    Before you start: save discipline

    Manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one before the final mission. No mission replay, no New Game+.

    Sources: outputlag; GTABase.

  2. 2

    Chapter 1 first

    Positive Mental Attitude: every meditation spot open in Chapter 1, above all Lucia’s balcony. Also start The Goodtime State — a plane clears the map fog fastest.

    Sources: GTABase; PowerPyx.

  3. 3

    Chapters 1–4 + Finale: the story

    The 12 story trophies unlock by finishing chapters and missions: Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory, and the final mission’s gold. Spoiler mode hides their names until you earn them.

    Sources: GamesRadar+; PC Gamer.

  4. 4

    Chapter 2+: partners & strangers

    Once you can switch characters: Hot Together and Retail Therapy with your partner, plus the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), activities, robberies and collectibles.

    Sources: GamesRadar+; PowerPyx.

  5. 5

    Free-roam clean-up

    250 stunts, 20 races, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 Top Fun wins, WAiNK at 100 spots, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles, money and property.

    Sources: GamesRadar+; PowerPyx.

  6. 6

    Post-story: the platinum

    A Long Road: the 100% checklist at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion (appears after the story; earlier progress counts; nothing on it is missable). Then Vice Got a Price! pops with the 54th trophy.

    Sources: PowerPyx; GamesRadar+; GTABase.
